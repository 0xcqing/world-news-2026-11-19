# World News · 19 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 poster summing up Thursday, 19 November 2026: a day of summitry and escalation. It covers the Xi–Putin–Trump trilateral meeting in Shenzhen on the sidelines of APEC; three oil tankers burning after mine strikes in the eastern Strait of Hormuz; a second Ukrainian strike on the Crimean Bridge alongside the EU's chemical-weapons warning to Moscow; an Israeli tunnel demolition west of the Yellow Line in Gaza; a US–Venezuela agreement restoring military-technical cooperation; and Fed minutes flagging higher rates while copper trades at its lowest since April — all wrapped in a ticker of shorter items from around the world.
